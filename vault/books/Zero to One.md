## There are different kinds of people:
1. Definte Optimist: Believe the future will be better than the present if they plan and work to build it.
2. Indefinite Optimist: Believe the future will get better, but have no concrete plans to making it so.
3. Definite pessimist: Believe the future can be known and expect it to get worse.
4. Indefinite pessimist: Anticipates a bleak future and sees no way to shape or fix it.

The argument: Be a **definite optimist**.
